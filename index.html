      Good bud guide

      Safety To the Arts

      Sprayed / Chem weed

      Sprayed:
      Chemical burn, residue, uneven colour or a harsh smell can be signs of spraying or chemical stress. Visual checks are clues only. Lab testing is the only proof.
      Clean:
      Even colour, intact structure and no powder, residue or chemical smell. A clean look is a good start, not a guarantee.

      Cut / Uncut

      Cut:
      Trimmed and dried flower. Fewer leaves and stems means less hiding space for pests or mould.
      Uncut:
      Whole plant or untrimmed branch. More leaf, stem and moisture, so inspect deeper for mould, mites and debris.

      Quality checks

      • Trichomes
        Cloudy, milky trichomes with a little amber usually look ripe. Clear can be early. Heavy amber can mean degraded THC.
      • Pests
        Check for webbing, black specks, tiny mites or insects, especially under leaves and deep inside buds.
      • Trim
        Tight trim with few sugar leaves. Loose leaf can hide pests, mould or a rough cure.
      • Cure
        Slow dry and cure. A hay smell, harsh smoke or a damp centre can mean it was rushed.
      • Structure
        Dense but springy buds. Rock hard, brittle or crumbly flower can be over dried or heavily processed.

      More checks

      • Smell Fresh, strain specific aroma. Ammonia, mildew or chemical smells are red flags.
      • Ash Clean grey white ash and smooth smoke are preferred. Black ash or harshness can point to a poor cure or contamination.
      • Mould Grey fuzz, white powder, brown patches or a musty smell. Do not use mouldy flower.
      • Lab test Ask for pesticide, mould and potency testing where legal. Visual checks cannot prove safety.
